## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS BY USE CASE

### For High-UV Environments (Tropical, South-Facing)
**Recommended:** EPDM (Bomar 8100 Series) or Silicone (NFM Tri-Matrix)
**Avoid:** Neoprene (moderate UV degradation), Foam (rapid degradation)
**Expected Replacement:** 15-20 years minimum

### For Continuous Saltwater Immersion (Liveaboards, Charter Vessels)
**Recommended:** EPDM, Silicone, Neoprene (all marine-grade)
**Best Practice:** Annual freshwater washdown, inspect annually
**Expected Replacement:** 10-15 years
**Suppliers:** Bomar, Beckson, New Found Metals (silicone advantage)

### For Budget-Conscious Applications
**Recommended:** Bomar by-the-foot (West Marine $6.49/ft), Taylor Made foam
**Trade-off:** Shorter lifespan (5-10 years) vs. premium options
**Cost Advantage:** 50-75% lower initial investment

### For Professional/Superyacht Applications
**Recommended:** NFM Tri-Matrix (silicone), Custom Alu-Star specifications, MAN engineering-grade
**Standards:** ISO 12217, CE 2013/53/EU compliance
**Documentation:** Full specification sheets, material certs, installation records
**Expected Replacement:** 20+ years
