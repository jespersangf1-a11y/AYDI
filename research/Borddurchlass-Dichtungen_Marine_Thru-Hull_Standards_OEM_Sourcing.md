## 1. INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS & REGULATIONS

### 1.1 ISO Standards (Primary Framework)

#### ISO 9093-1:1994 (Metallic Through-Hull Fittings)
- **Scope**: Small craft up to 24m hull length; metallic seacocks and thru-hulls
- **Thread Types**:
  - Cylindrical pipe threads (ISO 228-1)
  - Conical pipe threads (ISO 7-1)
- **Nominal Diameters Covered**: 1/4", 3/8", 1/2", 3/4", 1", 1¼", 1½", 2", 2½", 3", 4"
- **Materials**: Bronze (C83600), stainless steel (316L preferred)
- **Certification**: IMCI & Bureau Veritas approved

#### ISO 9093-2:2018 (Non-Metallic Through-Hull Fittings)
- **Scope**: Small craft up to 24m; composite/plastic thru-hulls and seacocks
- **Key Requirements**:
  - UV-stabilized materials; oxidation resistance
  - Smooth end finishes to prevent hose damage
  - Operating temperature: -20°C to +110°C (typical range)
- **Exclusions**: Engine exhaust fittings, sail drive connections
- **Standards Compliance**: Exceeds mechanical property requirements specified in ISO 150/188

#### ISO 9093:2020 (Unified Standard)
- **Status**: Current consolidated standard for all through-hull fittings (metallic & non-metallic)
- **Harmonizes**: Part 1 and Part 2 requirements into single document

---

### 1.2 ABYC Standards (American Boat & Yacht Council)

#### ABYC H-27: Seacocks, Thru-Hull Fittings, and Drain Plugs (2021 Edition)
- **Scope**: All boats; applicable to all penetrations at or below maximum heeled waterline
- **Key Mandatory Requirements**:
  - ALL below-waterline penetrations require seacock
  - Seacocks must meet ANSI/UL 1121
  - **Structural Strength**: Seacock + thru-hull assembly must withstand 500-pound static load at outboard end without failure
  - Above-waterline: reinforced piping acceptable; above static waterline only

- **Approved Seacock Types**:
  1. Flanged Sea Valve (integral flange, direct-to-hull mounting)
  2. In-Line Ball Valve (support entirely by thru-hull fitting)

- **Material Options**:
  - High-quality cast silicon bronze
  - Fiberglass-reinforced nylon (Marelon)
  - Internal: tapered bronze plug OR Teflon-coated plastic/stainless ball
  - Flange: bolted to hull structure

---

### 1.3 CE Marking Requirements (EU Recreational Craft Directive 2013/53/EU)

- **Scope**: Boats 2.5–24m sold in EU/EEA
- **Mandatory Documentation**:
  - CE marking on vessel
  - Declaration of Conformity
  - Technical file (design, materials, test reports)
- **Design Categories** (impact thru-hull specifications):
  - **Category A (Ocean)**: Wind >8 Beaufort, waves >4m → more robust thru-hull placement, backup seacocks
  - **Category B (Offshore)**: Wind ≤8, waves ≤4m
  - **Category C (Inshore)**: Wind ≤6, waves ≤2m
  - **Category D (Sheltered)**: Wind ≤4, waves ≤0.3m
- **Thirty Safety Headings** include: seacock placement, material certification, installation standards

---

### 1.4 Classification Society Requirements

#### Lloyd's Register (UK)
- Approves bronze seacocks (e.g., Blakes, Groco)
- Requires marine-grade DZR brass or bronze
- Type Approval available for OEM components

#### DNV-GL (Det Norske Veritas)
- Rules for classification of ships include watertight cable transits and sea chest specifications
- Thru-hull penetrations integrated into watertight bulkhead assessment
- Material thickness and sealing requirements

#### Bureau Veritas (France)
- Certifies TruDesign, Guidi, and other manufacturers to ISO 9093-2
- Enforces material testing and load certification

#### American Bureau of Shipping (ABS)
- US equivalent; type approval similar to DNV-GL
- Focus on load-bearing and structural integrity

---

### 1.5 Additional Regulatory Standards

#### ANSI/UL 1121 (Marine Through-Hull Fittings and Sea-Valves)
- US standard; cross-referenced in ABYC H-27
- Test requirements: pressure, structural load, corrosion resistance

#### AS/NZS 1660 (Australian/New Zealand Standard)
- Limited public information; specific component requirements not widely published
- Applied to recreation craft in AUS/NZ markets

#### US Coast Guard (33 CFR Part 181)
- Federal manufacturing requirements
- Incorporates ABYC and UL standards
- Seacock requirement: same as ABYC H-27

## 6. DETAILED SPECIFICATIONS BY ISO 9093 STANDARD

### 6.1 Thread Specifications

#### Cylindrical Pipe Threads (ISO 228-1)
- **Thread Form**: Parallel threads; straight profile
- **Pitch**: 1.814 mm per thread (standard metric conversion)
- **Nominal Sizes Covered**:
  - M10 × 1 (equiv. 3/8")
  - M14 × 1.5 (equiv. ½")
  - M18 × 1.5 (equiv. ¾")
  - M22 × 1.5 (equiv. 1")
  - M30 × 2 (equiv. 1¼")
  - M36 × 2 (equiv. 1½")
  - M48 × 2 (equiv. 2")

#### Conical Pipe Threads (ISO 7-1, Taper)
- **Thread Form**: Conical taper (1:16 ratio)
- **Pitch**: Varies by size
- **Applications**: High-pressure sealing; metal-to-metal contact preferred

#### NPS/NPSM (National Pipe Straight Mechanical, US Standard)
- **Thread Form**: Parallel (non-tapered)
- **Pitch**: Standard US Unified (varies by size)
- **Sizes**: ¼", 3/8", ½", ¾", 1", 1¼", 1½", 2", 2½", 3", 4"
- **Flange Diameter Range**: 1.6"–4.5" depending on thru-hull size

#### NPT (National Pipe Thread, Tapered, Obsolete for Marine)
- **Note**: ABYC & ISO now specify NPSM (parallel) for marine applications
- **Issue**: NPT + NPS thread mismatch can occur with older inventory

---

### 6.2 Dimensional Requirements

#### Flange Design
- **Material**: Machined steel or bronze; grooved to retain sealant compound
- **Diameter**: 1.5"–4.5" depending on thru-hull size
- **Thickness**: 0.25"–0.375"
- **Fastening**: Typically 4 bolt holes (M6–M10 or ¼"–⅜" fasteners)
- **Bolt Pattern**: Square or circular; ~2.5"–3.5" bolt circle diameter

#### Installation Engagement
- **Through-Hull Projection**: ½"–1¼" beyond backing block (internal hull side)
- **Thread Engagement**: Minimum 5 full threads (Forespar spec)
- **Tighten Torque**: 20 ft-lbs (Marelon); 30–50 ft-lbs (bronze)

#### Hose Connection Interfaces
- **Barb Types**: Single barb, double barb, or smooth fitting
- **Hose I.D.**: ½", ⅝", ¾", 1", 1¼", 1½", 2"
- **Hose Clamp**: Stainless steel, two-clamp minimum (ABYC H-27)

---

### 6.3 Material Standards & Corrosion Resistance

#### Bronze Composition (C83600, Most Common)
- Cu (copper): ~83%
- Sn (tin): ~10%
- Zn (zinc): ~5%
- Pb (lead): ~2%
- **Corrosion Class**: Excellent in seawater; dezincification risk if zinc >% threshold

#### Stainless Steel (316L Preferred for Salt Water)
- Ni (nickel): 10–14%
- Mo (molybdenum): 2–3%
- Cr (chromium): 16–18%
- **Corrosion Class**: Superior to 304 stainless; resistant to pitting in seawater
- **Note**: 304 stainless NOT recommended for marine thru-hulls

#### DZR Brass (De-Zincification Resistant)
- Similar to C83600 but with corrosion inhibitor additives
- Prevents zinc leaching in seawater
- Lloyd's & MOD approved (Blakes standard)

#### Marelon (Composite)
- Polyamide resin (reinforced with glass fiber)
- **Corrosion**: Absolute immunity to galvanic corrosion & electrolysis
- **UV Stability**: Excellent (marine-grade additives)
- **Strength**: Exceeds ISO 9093-2 mechanical requirements
- **Temperature**: -40°C to +110°C operating range

## 7. CRITICAL INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS (ABYC H-27 & ISO 9093)

### 7.1 Seacock Placement Rules

- **Below Maximum Heeled Waterline**: Seacock MANDATORY
- **Above Static Waterline**: Seacock optional; reinforced hose acceptable
- **Sailboat Bilge Pump Discharge**: If below max heeled waterline → seacock required
- **Engine Intakes**: Always seacock below waterline

### 7.2 Structural Support

- **Backing Block**: Required for all below-waterline penetrations
  - Material: Marine plywood (BS 1088) or solid wood; min. 1.5× thru-hull diameter thickness
  - Area: Support flange with 2–4" margin all around
  - Fastening: Through-bolts or lag screws into hull structure

- **Load Test**: 500 lbs static load at outboard fitting end (ABYC H-27)

### 7.3 Sealing Compound & Hose Clamps

- **Bedding**: Sikaflex 291, 3M 5200, or equivalent polyurethane sealant
- **Hose Clamps**: Stainless steel (316L minimum); two-clamp minimum per ABYC
- **Clamp Torque**: Hand-tight + ¼ turn (avoid over-tightening composite)

### 7.4 Bonding & Cathodic Protection

- **Bonding Wire**: All below-waterline thru-hulls bonded to copper bonding bar
- **Wire Gauge**: #6 AWG green (Nordhavn spec); #8 minimum per ABYC
- **Zinc Anodes**: 3× 2.5"×5.75" plates typical for large vessels (Nordhavn); sized per displacement

## 9. TROUBLESHOOTING & COMMON ISSUES

### Osmotic Blistering (Composite Thru-Hulls)
- **Cause**: Water absorption into fiber-glass interface
- **Prevention**: UV protection; sealant integrity
- **Remedy**: UV-protective paint or varnish over Marelon/TruDesign

### Dezincification (Bronze)
- **Cause**: Zinc leaching in brass compositions
- **Prevention**: Use C83600 or DZR brass; avoid 400-series brasses
- **Indicator**: White, crumbly deposits around threads
- **Remedy**: Replace fitting; switch to DZR or stainless

### Thread Corrosion & Seizing
- **Cause**: Galvanic action; saltwater exposure
- **Prevention**: Use marine sealant compound; isolate dissimilar metals
- **Remedy**: Remove with penetrating oil (WD-40, PB Blaster); use anti-seize on replacement

### Hose Separation
- **Cause**: Single clamp; improper torque
- **Prevention**: Two stainless clamps per ABYC; periodic inspection
- **Remedy**: Re-secure with new clamps; inspect hose condition
