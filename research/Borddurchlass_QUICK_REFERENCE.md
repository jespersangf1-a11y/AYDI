## QUICK-SELECT THRU-HULL GUIDE

### Above Waterline (Non-Critical)
- **First Choice**: TruDesign (cost + ease)
- **Premium**: Vetus stainless
- **Load Rating**: Structural (not required) but verify hose security

### Below Waterline (MANDATORY Seacock)
- **First Choice**: Forespar 93 Series (Marelon) or Groco bronze seacock
- **Budget**: Forespar + basic ball valve
- **Premium**: Integrated Groco seacock assembly
- **Material Selection**:
  - Saltwater: 316L stainless or DZR brass (avoid 304)
  - Bronze: C83600 or DZR (check dezincification specs)

### Critical Systems (Engine Intake, Galley Sink)
- **Requirement**: Seacock + backing block + bonding wire
- **Material**: Groco bronze or Forespar Marelon
- **Redundancy**: Consider second shut-off valve for safety

## INSTALLATION CHECKLIST

- [ ] **Below WL?** → Seacock + flanged thru-hull required
- [ ] **Backing Block**: Marine plywood BS 1088, 1.5× diameter thickness
- [ ] **Fasteners**: Stainless steel, corrosion-resistant
- [ ] **Hose Clamps**: Stainless, two minimum per ABYC H-27
- [ ] **Sealant**: Sikaflex 291, 3M 5200, or polyurethane marine grade
- [ ] **Torque**: 20 ft-lbs (Marelon); 30–50 ft-lbs (bronze)
- [ ] **Thread Engagement**: Minimum 5 full threads
- [ ] **Bonding Wire**: #6 AWG green (commercial/superyacht)
- [ ] **Labels**: Function label on visible handle/valve

## RED FLAGS & DEFECTS TO AUDIT

### Defects Indicating Poor Quality
- ❌ Plastic (non-marine-grade) thru-hulls below WL
- ❌ Single hose clamp per connection
- ❌ Missing backing block or inadequate support
- ❌ Visible corrosion/white deposits (dezincification)
- ❌ No seacock below waterline (non-compliant ABYC)
- ❌ NPT thread (tapered) without proper adapter
- ❌ Through-bolt fastening missing on large penetrations

### Minor Issues (Acceptable with Documentation)
- ✓ Small orange-peel gelcoat around thru-hull (cosmetic)
- ✓ Sealant squeeze-out (trim flush, acceptable)
- ✓ Bonding wire not visible (may be routed internally)

## INTEGRATION NOTES FOR AYDI

### Module Application: **Compliance & Materials**

**Compliance Check** (CE/ABYC/Lloyd's):
1. Verify design category (A/B/C/D)
2. Check boat length (scope: 2.5–24m)
3. Below-WL penetration count
4. Seacock presence + material certification
5. Backing block documentation

**Materials Check** (Corrosion & UV Risk):
1. Material identification (bronze, stainless, composite)
2. Saltwater vs. freshwater environment
3. UV exposure (deck, cabin-top thru-hulls)
4. Galvanic coupling risk (dissimilar metals)
5. Maintenance record (corrosion history)

**Cost Estimation**:
- Parametric model: Base cost per fitting size + seacock premium + installation labor (€200–500 per installation)
