## KEY FINDINGS SUMMARY

### Regulatory Framework
- **ISO 9093:2020** is the current unified standard for all thru-hull fittings (2.5–24m craft)
- **ABYC H-27 (2021)** mandates seacock for ALL below-waterline penetrations in USA/international
- **CE Marking (2013/53/EU)** requires design category assignment; thru-hull placement critical
- **Classification societies** (Lloyd's, DNV-GL, ABS) require type approval and material certification
- **500 lbs structural load rating** is non-negotiable (ABYC H-27)

### OEM Supplier Hierarchy

**Tier 1 (Premium, Full Specification)**
- TruDesign (New Zealand composite; ISO certified)
- Forespar Marelon (USA; exceeds ISO 9093-2)
- Vetus (Netherlands; trusted by German builders)

**Tier 2 (Production Standard)**
- Groco (USA; 100-year history; bronze seacocks)
- Perko (USA; traditional production boats)
- Blakes (UK; heritage marine)

**Tier 3 (OEM/Superyacht)**
- Guidi (Italy; 4,200-product catalog; premium builders)
- Hallberg-Rassy custom suppliers (stainless, high-grade)

### Material Selection by Application

| Application | Best Choice | Material | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Above Waterline** | TruDesign 90xxx | Composite | Cost + ease; no seacock needed |
| **Below Waterline (Standard)** | Forespar 93 Series | Marelon + integrated seacock | Single assembly; easy replacement |
| **Below Waterline (Premium)** | Groco TH series + seacock | Bronze | Traditional; repairable; excellent corrosion resistance |
| **Critical Systems** | Groco seacock assembly | Bronze | Full control; high load rating; in-water serviceable |
| **Superyacht** | Guidi or custom | Stainless/bronze | Engineering precision; redundant systems |

### Global Sourcing Map

**Strongest Regional Supply Chains**:
1. **Germany**: SVB (OEM parts authority); TOPLICHT; direct access to European builders
2. **UK**: Force4 (40-year reputation); traditional marine sector
3. **USA**: Defender, West Marine (broad selection); technical support
4. **France**: Accastillage Diffusion (equivalent to West Marine); Mediterranean supply
5. **Asia-Pacific**: Poseidon, Arc Marine (Singapore hub); growing availability

**Lead Times**: 2–4 weeks (stock); 4–8 weeks (OEM integrated seacock).

**Pricing Range**:
- Composite thru-hull: €12–120 (TruDesign/Forespar)
- Bronze seacock: €45–436 (Groco, Blakes)
- Integrated assembly: €130–500+ (premium options)
- Regional variation: EUR 15–25 premium over USD equivalent

## SOURCING WORKFLOW EXAMPLE

**Scenario**: Sourcing thru-hulls for a 14m Bénéteau sailboat (production class, 6 below-waterline penetrations)

**Step 1: Identify OEM Supplier**
- Reference: Quick Reference → OEM Suppliers table
- Result: Bénéteau primary supplier = TruDesign; backup = Vetus
- Action: Contact SVB (OEM parts) or Bénéteau dealer

**Step 2: Verify Part Numbers**
- Reference: Full document, Section 3.3 (TruDesign)
- Result: 90xxx series; part numbers 90424, 90426 (typical)
- Action: Request specific part numbers from Bénéteau technical manual

**Step 3: Compliance Check**
- Reference: Full document, Section 7 (ABYC H-27 Checklist)
- Result: All 6 penetrations below WL → all require integrated seacock
- Decision: Upgrade from simple thru-hull to seacock assembly

**Step 4: Select Material & Supplier**
- Reference: Quick Reference → Material Comparison matrix
- Options:
  - Budget: TruDesign simple thru-hull + separate ball valve (€12–20 + €15–30)
  - Recommended: Forespar 93 Series seacock (€130–200, integrated)
  - Premium: Groco bronze seacock assembly (€300–400, traditional)
- Action: Consult with Bénéteau/boat designer on OEM standard

**Step 5: Source Globally**
- Reference: Full document, Section 4 (Worldwide Sourcing)
- Options by region:
  - Germany: SVB (OEM parts) or TOPLICHT
  - USA: Defender or West Marine (fastest delivery if sourcing from North America)
  - France: Accastillage Diffusion or Uship
- Action: Solicit quotes from 2–3 regional distributors; compare lead times

**Step 6: Procurement**
- Lead time: 2–4 weeks (stock), 4–8 weeks (OEM custom)
- Cost: 6 seacock assemblies × €150 average = €900 + shipping
- Bulk discount: 20–35% possible on order >5 units
- Installation labor: €200–500 per unit (backing block, bonding, testing)
