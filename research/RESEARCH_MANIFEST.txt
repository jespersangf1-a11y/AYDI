IDENTIFIED RESEARCH GAPS
================================================================================

1. Custom Builder Specifications
   - Oyster, Swan, Contest, Amel thru-hull specs not publicly available
   - Recommendation: Contact builders directly or access via SVB

2. Asian Sourcing Detail
   - Limited documentation beyond Singapore (Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand)
   - Recommendation: Direct outreach to regional marine distributors

3. EUR Real-Time Pricing
   - Requires quarterly updates; VAT regional variation
   - Recommendation: Solicit direct distributor quotes

4. Lead Time Consistency
   - Varies by supplier, season, and product
   - Recommendation: Maintain supplier quote database

5. Failure Rate Data
   - No public marine casualty database linked to thru-hull defects
   - Recommendation: Survey marine surveyors for failure patterns

6. DNV-GL/ABS Detailed Specs
   - Proprietary classification society databases
   - Recommendation: Contact classification societies directly
